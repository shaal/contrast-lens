Read the signal

Two lenses.
One clear decision.

WCAG gives you a familiar ratio. APCA adds perception, polarity, and type size. Use both as a conversation starter — then look at the actual interface.

01
Aa

WCAG 2 ratio

The shared baseline. A 4.5:1 ratio is the AA target for normal text; 7:1 is the stricter AAA target.

Good for compliance language
02
Lc

APCA score

A perceptual, directional score. Lc 60 is a useful body-text guide; polarity tells you whether light or dark is doing the work.

Good for type-aware decisions
